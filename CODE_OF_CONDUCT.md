# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback with care.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people using the firmware.

Harassment, sexualized language, personal attacks, trolling, insults, and the
publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's Security tab.
Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban a contributor for harmful behavior.
