Update your radio

Install or update ApexTX on your FlySky Noble NB4 or NB4+. Everything runs locally and is verified on the radio.

This replaces the complete firmware including the bootloader — for first installation or recovery. The radio must be in STM32 ROM DFU mode (hold the recessed grip button while switching it on). Your current firmware is backed up first.
